characters:
- protagonist
- best friend/sidekick
- rival
- comic relief
- antagonist

overall idea - the characters are all locked behind their character archetypes and the user's character spawns into the world.

main story:
chapter 1 - regular school day, user meets characters.
chapter 2 - something's off. player starts getting story choices that shouldn't exist, characters acting weird.
chapter 3 - player has two choices at the end: continue story as written or break archetypes and see what happens (2 possible endings).

variables/stats:
- trust (different for each character)
   - if >= 4: unlocks side story
- curiosity
   - if >= 3: unlocks hidden dialogue
- defiance
   - if >= 5: unlocks alternate ending (GAME OVER)
- discoveries
   - counter as story progresses
